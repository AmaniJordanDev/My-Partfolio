Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about my services

What's your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary based on complexity. Typically, a basic website takes 1-2 weeks, while more complex projects may take 3-6 weeks or longer. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes! I offer various support packages depending on your needs. From 30 days of free support included in projects to ongoing maintenance plans, I'm here to help after launch.

What's your payment process?

I typically require a 50% deposit to start the project, with the remaining balance due upon completion. We can discuss payment terms that work best for your situation.

Do you work with startups?

Absolutely! I love working with startups and have flexible pricing options. Let's discuss your vision and budget to create something amazing together.

Can you help with existing websites?

Yes! I can redesign, rebuild, or improve existing websites. Whether it's a complete overhaul or specific updates, I'll help bring your vision to life.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Of course! Every website I build is fully responsive and mobile-optimized. Your site will look and function beautifully on all devices.
